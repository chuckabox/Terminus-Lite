# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and other developers browsing a portfolio. They are looking for technical competence, clean architecture, and clear documentation.

## Product Purpose
A distributed sub-agent router that solves context-window bloat by offloading log summarization to local SLMs. It demonstrates an understanding of distributed systems, AI agent workflows, and local LLM orchestration (Ollama).

## Brand Personality
Professional, technical, and understated. It should feel like a high-quality open-source utility, not a commercial product.

## Anti-references
- AI-slop marketing pages with excessive emojis and "vibrant" language.
- Overly complex documentation that hides the core logic.
- Generic AI-generated project templates.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Hype**: Focus on how it works and how to use it, not just what it "promises."
- **Technical Precision**: Use accurate terminology (distributed, context-bloat, distillation) without fluff.
- **Scannable Architecture**: The "why" and "how" should be immediately visible through clear headings and diagrams.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Clean, high-contrast typography and semantic HTML in the dashboard. Standard markdown formatting for documentation to ensure readability across all viewers.
